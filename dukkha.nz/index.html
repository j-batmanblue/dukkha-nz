DUKKHA.NZ IS STILL IN DEVELOPMENT

DUKKHA is an emerging underground alternative rock band hailing from the coastal town of Mount Maunganui in Tauranga, New Zealand. Originally formed by frontman and guitarist Cameron Orchard alongside drummer Joel Brown, the band set out to shine a spotlight on the seaside city's vibrant local music scene. Their lineup evolved to feature Flynn Augustine on bass and backing vocals, adding a richer dynamic to their heavy, alternative rock sound.
